Common questions

How do I open a protected photo?

Open the photo, tap •••, choose Continue Securely and enter the password.

I entered the password and nothing happened.

A wrong password intentionally leaves the preview on screen without an error. Check the password and try again.

I forgot my password.

There is no password recovery. The original cannot be recovered by anyone, including the developer.

Why does another app show only a small or blurred image?

That is the protected photo's preview. Other apps never see the original.

Does Photos upload my pictures?

No. Photos has no server and no account. See the privacy policy.
