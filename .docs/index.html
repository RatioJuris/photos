Your photos, calm and private.

An iOS-style photo gallery for Android, with photos you can lock behind a password that still look like ordinary pictures to everyone else.

Version 1.0.0 · Android 12+ · No account · No cloud · No tracking · Free and open source

Screenshots

What you can do

Browse beautifully

Years, Months and All Photos, pinch-to-resize grid, albums, search, and a fluid viewer with swipe gestures.

Protect individual photos

Argon2id + AES-256-GCM encryption on your phone. Protected photos look like any other photo, and other apps see only a preview.

Archive and Bin

Hide photos without deleting them, and recover deleted items for up to 30 days.

Places

See where photos were taken. Locations are read on the device, and maps stay off until you turn them on.

Videos too

Videos play right in the viewer. Protection is for photos only.

Private by design

No account, no analytics, no ads. Passwords are never stored.

There is no password recovery. If you forget a password, the protected original cannot be recovered by anyone — including the developer.
